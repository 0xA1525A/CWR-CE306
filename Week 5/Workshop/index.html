King Hunt is a custom chess variant for learning and fun.

Keyboard: Tab to a square · Arrow keys navigate · Enter/Space selects or moves.

Quick guide

How to play

  1. You control the white pieces and always move first.
  2. Select a piece, then click a highlighted square. You can also drag and drop a piece.
  3. Pieces move using familiar chess movement. Sliding pieces cannot jump over other pieces. Pawns can promote to a queen on the last rank.
  4. No check or checkmate: kings are not protected from attack. You win only when you capture the black king; you lose if the bot captures your king.
  5. Use the level buttons to change the bot’s strategy. You can restart at any time.

This variant intentionally ignores check, checkmate, castling and en passant. The bot uses a small local heuristic, so no API key or account is required.
